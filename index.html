How this works: Basic = Component ÷ 186.667%, Allowances = Basic ÷ 60% × 40%, SSF (20% of Basic) - these plus Incentive always reconcile to Gross. SSF Contribution (31% of annual Basic - Employer 20% + Employee 11%) drives the retirement-fund deduction and "SSF deposited per month." Taxable Income then runs through the SST/Remuneration Tax rules above, which are editable in the config menu so they can be updated for future fiscal years without touching the code.
